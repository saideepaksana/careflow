## What to expect

After pneumonia, the fever usually settles within a week. Chest pain eases within 4 weeks, the cough and phlegm reduce over 6 weeks, and tiredness can last up to 3 months.

## Clearing phlegm

Breathe in deeply, hold for a few seconds, and then give a strong cough. Do this several times a day. Drinking enough and breathing steam can loosen the phlegm.

## Preventing spread

Cover your mouth when you cough, wash your hands often, and throw used tissues away straight away.

## Vaccines

The pneumococcal vaccine and the yearly flu vaccine lower the chance of another pneumonia. Ask your primary care doctor whether you need them.

## Why the follow-up X-ray matters

The follow-up chest X-ray checks that the lung has cleared completely. This is especially important for people over 50 and for smokers.
