## Low blood sugar

Low blood sugar (hypoglycaemia) is a reading below 70 mg/dL. Signs are shaking, sweating, hunger and confusion. Take 15 g of fast sugar, such as half a glass of fruit juice or three glucose tablets, then check again after 15 minutes. Repeat if it is still low, then eat a snack.

## High blood sugar

Signs of high blood sugar are thirst, passing a lot of urine, tiredness and blurred vision. Drink water, take your medicines as prescribed, and check your sugar more often.

## Sick days

When you are ill, your sugar can rise even if you eat less. Keep drinking fluids, check your sugar every 4 hours, and call your nurse line if you are vomiting or cannot keep food down.

## Exercise

Walking for 30 minutes on most days lowers blood sugar and helps your medicine work better. Carry a snack in case your sugar drops.

## Feet

Diabetes can reduce feeling in the feet, so small injuries go unnoticed. Never walk barefoot, keep your feet clean and dry, and cut toenails straight across.

## HbA1c

HbA1c shows your average blood sugar over the past three months. For most people the target is below 7 percent. It is checked every three months until your sugars are steady.
