## Fasting before blood tests

Most blood tests do not need fasting. The basic metabolic panel and the HbA1c test can be done at any time of day. A fasting lipid (cholesterol) test needs 10 to 12 hours without food; water is allowed. Your booking confirmation says if fasting is needed.

## Medicines on the day of a test

Take your usual medicines on the day of a blood test unless your doctor has told you otherwise. Bring a list of your medicines.

## Where to go

The lab is on the ground floor of the outpatient building, next to the pharmacy. Radiology, for X-rays and scans, is on the first floor.

## Chest X-ray

A chest X-ray needs no preparation. You will be asked to remove jewellery and clothing with metal above the waist. It takes about 10 minutes.

## Getting results

Most blood test results are ready within 2 working days and go straight to your care team. Your team will contact you if any result needs action, or discuss it at your next visit.
