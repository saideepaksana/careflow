## Follow-up visits

See the cardiology clinic within 14 days of discharge. No blood tests are needed before that visit.

## Medicines

Take furosemide 40 mg once a day in the morning.

## Warning signs

Call the nurse line the same day if your weight goes up by more than 3 kg in a week. Call 112 for chest pain or severe breathlessness at rest.

## Fluids and salt

Limit fluids to 2 litres a day. Keep salt low.

## Driving

Do not drive for 4 weeks after discharge.
