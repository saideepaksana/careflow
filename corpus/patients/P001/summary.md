## Reason for admission

Ravi Kumar was admitted with increasing breathlessness, swollen ankles and a weight gain of 4 kg over ten days. This was caused by worsening heart failure, where the heart does not pump strongly enough and fluid builds up in the lungs and legs.

## Hospital stay

He was treated with water tablets through a drip, which removed about 4 kg of extra fluid. His breathing improved and the ankle swelling went down. An echocardiogram (heart ultrasound) showed the heart pumping at 35 percent of normal strength. A chest X-ray showed fluid that cleared before discharge. Kidney blood tests stayed stable.

## Condition at discharge

At discharge he could walk around the ward without stopping, his breathing was comfortable at rest, and his weight was 72 kg. This is his target "dry weight" to compare home readings against.
