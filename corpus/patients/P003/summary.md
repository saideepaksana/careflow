## Reason for admission

Arjun Reddy was admitted with very high blood sugar (hyperglycaemia) of 450 mg/dL, extreme thirst, passing a lot of urine, and dehydration. He had stopped taking his diabetes tablets two weeks earlier.

## Hospital stay

He was given fluids through a drip and insulin until his sugar levels came down. Once his sugars were steady, he was switched back to metformin tablets. His HbA1c, which shows average sugar over three months, was 10.2 percent.

## Condition at discharge

At discharge he was eating and drinking normally, and his fasting sugar was 140 mg/dL.
