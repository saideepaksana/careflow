## Follow-up visits

See the endocrinology clinic within 14 days of discharge to review your sugar readings and your medicine.

Have an HbA1c blood test within 30 days of discharge. You do not need to fast for this test.

## Medicines

Take metformin 500 mg twice a day, with breakfast and with your evening meal. Taking it with food reduces stomach upset. Do not stop it without talking to your care team, because stopping it led to this admission.

## Sugar checks

Check your blood sugar twice a day: before breakfast, and two hours after your evening meal. Write the readings in your logbook and bring it to every visit. A good fasting reading for you is between 80 and 130 mg/dL.

## Warning signs

Call the nurse line the same day if your blood sugar is above 300 mg/dL.

Call 112 straight away for fainting or confusion.

## Eating

Eat three regular meals a day and do not skip breakfast. Fill half your plate with vegetables, keep rice or roti to a quarter of the plate, and avoid sweets, sugary drinks and fruit juice.

## Foot care

Check both feet every evening for cuts, blisters or redness. Wear footwear indoors and outdoors. Show any sore that does not heal in a few days to the nurse.
