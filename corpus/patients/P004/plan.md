## Follow-up visits

See the orthopedics clinic within 14 days of discharge. Your staples will be removed and the surgeon will check the wound and how you are walking.

See your primary care doctor within 30 days of discharge for a general review.

## Medicines

Take apixaban 2.5 mg twice a day, morning and evening, for 35 days after the operation. It is a blood thinner that prevents clots in the legs and lungs. You may bruise more easily while taking it.

Take paracetamol for pain as needed, up to 1 g four times a day.

## Hip precautions

For the first 6 weeks, do not bend your hip past 90 degrees, do not cross your legs, and do not twist on the operated leg. Use a raised toilet seat and a high, firm chair. Sleep on your back with a pillow between your knees.

## Wound care

Keep the wound dry and covered. You may shower after 5 days using a waterproof dressing. Do not take baths or go swimming until the surgeon says the wound has healed.

## Warning signs

Call the nurse line the same day if your temperature is above 38 C, or if the wound becomes red, hot or starts to leak.

Call 112 straight away for sudden leg swelling with chest pain.

## Walking and driving

Use the walker at all times for the first 4 weeks, then the surgeon will decide whether you can move to a stick. Do not drive for 6 weeks after the operation.
