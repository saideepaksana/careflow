## Reason for admission

Fatima Shaikh was admitted for a planned total replacement of her right hip, because of severe osteoarthritis that caused pain when walking and at night.

## Operation and hospital stay

The operation went without complications. The new hip joint was placed through an incision on the side of the hip, and the wound was closed with staples. She started walking with a walker on the day after surgery and practised stairs with the physiotherapist before going home.

## Condition at discharge

At discharge she could walk with a walker, manage one flight of stairs, and get in and out of bed on her own. Her pain was controlled with paracetamol.
