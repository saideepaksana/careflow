## Reason for admission

Joseph Mathew was admitted with a high fever, a cough with yellow phlegm and pain on breathing in. A chest X-ray showed pneumonia in the lower part of the right lung.

## Hospital stay

He was given antibiotics through a drip for two days and oxygen for one day. His fever settled on the second day, and he was switched to antibiotic tablets.

## Condition at discharge

At discharge he had no fever for 24 hours, his oxygen level was 96 percent on room air, and he was eating and drinking normally.
