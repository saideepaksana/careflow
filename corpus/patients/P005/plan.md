## Follow-up visits

See your primary care doctor within 7 days of discharge to check that the infection is clearing.

Have a chest X-ray within 30 days of discharge to make sure the lung has cleared.

## Medicines

Take amoxicillin 500 mg three times a day for 5 days. Finish the whole course even if you feel better, so the infection does not come back. Take paracetamol for fever or chest pain as needed.

## Warning signs

Call the nurse line the same day if your temperature is above 38.5 C.

Call 112 straight away for severe difficulty breathing.

## Rest and fluids

Rest as much as you need for the first week. Drink plenty of fluids, about 2 to 3 litres a day, to help loosen the phlegm.

## Recovery and work

The cough may last 3 to 6 weeks and tiredness may last a month. You can return to a desk job after about a week if you have had no fever for 3 days. Return to physical work after the primary care visit.

## Smoking

Do not smoke. Smoking slows lung healing and makes pneumonia more likely to come back.
