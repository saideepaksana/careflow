## Reason for admission

Lakshmi Menon was admitted with a flare-up of her COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease), set off by a chest infection. She was breathless at rest, coughing up green phlegm, and her oxygen level was 85 percent.

## Hospital stay

She was treated with nebulisers, a five-day course of steroid tablets, antibiotics and oxygen. Her breathing improved over four days and the oxygen was stopped on the third day. The steroid and antibiotic courses were completed in hospital.

## Condition at discharge

At discharge her oxygen level was 93 percent on room air, and she could walk to the bathroom and back without stopping.
